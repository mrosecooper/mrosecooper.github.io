Hello! if youre here, ill assume youre interested in learning cinch cipher (or youre just curious, in which case youre free to stay). this page will be a (hopefully) foolproof guide to learning cinch cipher!

now, i named this thing cinch because, in my opinion, its the easiest one of my ciphers. its also the first one i created! i made it after learning one of the ciphers from the game splatoon and thinking it was too easy for people to read as it kept similar letter shapes to the latin alphabet, so i made my own!

cinch cipher was made in 2022 and over time has gotten very very simplified. one of the first variations--which i like to call CCC (complicated cinch cipher)--was small dots arranged on a 3 by 3 cube. as you can imagine, that type of layout made it very irritating to distinguish the different letters, and so i played a game of connect the dots to get much easier symbols to both read and write.
